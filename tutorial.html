Field Manual
Round 1

Loop

  1. Draw to fill open slots
  2. Place your row
  3. Resolve in placement order
  4. Repeat until one side runs dry

Value

  • Value is both HP and roll ceiling
  • Value 7 means a roll from 1 to 7
  • Higher value is wider range, not certainty

Approach

  • 🔥 Pressure beats 🧭 Positioning
  • 🧭 Positioning beats 🎭 Appeal
  • 🎭 Appeal beats 🔥 Pressure
  • Winner rolls twice and keeps the higher result
  • Tie: lower roll hits both cards

Roles

  • Attack adds +1 to its roll
  • Defense reduces incoming damage by 1
  • Support adds +1 range to adjacent allies

Read The Board

  • 1st, 2nd, 3rd markers show fight order
  • Destroyed cards move to the Cemetery
  • Hover cards to inspect roll ranges
