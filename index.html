Round 1

Flow

  1. Draw cards
  2. Place all cards
  3. Resolve combat
  4. Repeat

Combat

  • Slots fight in the order you placed your cards
  • Value is both HP and the dice ceiling
  • Approach winner rolls twice and keeps the higher roll
  • Approach loser rolls once
  • Approach tie: both roll once, lower roll deals shared damage
  • Attack adds +1 to the rolled value
  • Defense reduces incoming damage by 1
  • Support adds +1 to adjacent allies' roll ceiling

Value

  • Cards use values from 3 to 10
  • Higher value means a wider roll range
  • Support can widen the range temporarily before combat

Fight Order

  • 1st, 2nd, and 3rd markers show the combat order
  • The order follows your placement sequence

Approach

  • 🔥 Pressure beats 🧭 Positioning
  • 🧭 Positioning beats 🎭 Appeal
  • 🎭 Appeal beats 🔥 Pressure
